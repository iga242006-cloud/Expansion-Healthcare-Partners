What We Offer

Services Built Around How Healthcare Actually Grows

In a rapidly changing medical environment, it has become critical to work just as hard "on" your practice as "in" your practice.

EHP offers three core service areas — each customizable in scope, duration, and cost to match your organization's specific situation. We work with individual providers, specialty groups, and large hospital systems across five states.

Service 01

Medical Practice Business Development

Specialists and hospitals hire us to build their business. Our proven process uncovers barriers to referral, connects you with the providers you need to know, and tracks referral data to ensure growth.

We embed ourselves in your community, identify the right referral sources, and build the relationships that translate into consistent, trackable referral volume. This isn't a one-time campaign — it's an ongoing strategic partnership.

  • Referral barrier identification and elimination
  • Targeted physician outreach and relationship development
  • Referral data tracking and reporting
  • Service line marketing and positioning
  • Community event coordination and presence
  • Ongoing competitive market intelligence

Service 02

Physician Onboarding & Marketing

Our deep understanding of the referral process and knowledge of referral sources proves invaluable to new physicians establishing their practice within an existing group.

A new physician joining a group has a narrow window to establish their presence and build referral volume. EHP accelerates that process by leveraging our existing relationships and systematic introduction strategy — so new providers aren't starting from zero.

  • Community introduction and relationship-building
  • Referral source mapping and prioritization
  • New provider announcement and outreach campaigns
  • Integration with existing group referral networks
  • Early-stage referral volume tracking
  • Positioning strategy for specialty differentiation

Service 03

Physician Placement & Employment Contract Consulting

EHP assists physicians in job search, placement, and contract negotiation — including site visits, community engagement, financial consultation, and legal referral for contract assistance.

Finding the right position is only part of the challenge. Understanding the contract, the community, and the culture of a prospective employer requires experience and objectivity that few physicians have access to. We provide that perspective.

  • Physician job search strategy and market assessment
  • Employer identification and opportunity matching
  • Site visit coordination and community evaluation
  • Employment contract review and negotiation support
  • Financial terms consultation and benchmarking
  • Legal referral for contract legal assistance
